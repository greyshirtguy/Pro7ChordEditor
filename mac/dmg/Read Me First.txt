Pro7 Chord Editor for the Mac
An early build. It needs macOS 15 or later.


WHAT IT IS

It puts chords on the songs of a ProPresenter library, so that ProPresenter
shows them on a stage screen. It opens the library where it lies and changes
the songs' own files.

It is one person's hobby. Nobody supports it and nothing is promised. It is not
ProPresenter and has nothing to do with Renewed Vision, who make ProPresenter.


BEFORE YOU USE IT

- Keep a backup of your ProPresenter library.
- Quit ProPresenter first. Nothing is saved while ProPresenter is running,
  unless that is allowed in Settings, under Advanced: read the warning there
  before allowing it.
- Try it on a copy first: copy one folder out of ProPresenter's Libraries to
  somewhere else, and open the copy with Choose Folder.

Every change is saved as it is made. There is no Save. Before the app first
changes a song's file it keeps a copy of the file as it was; the copies are
under Earlier Versions, in a song's header, and in Settings under Backups.
They are kept on this Mac only, in
~/Library/Application Support/Pro7ChordEditor


OPENING IT THE FIRST TIME

This build is not signed with an Apple developer certificate, and Apple has
not checked it. So macOS will not open it at a double-click. To open it:

1. Drag Pro7 Chord Editor to Applications, and double-click it there.
   macOS says it could not verify the app. Close that message.
2. Open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, and scroll down to
   Security. It says Pro7 Chord Editor was blocked. Click Open Anyway,
   and say yes when it asks again.

After that it opens like any other app.

Do this only for something whose maker you have reason to trust. What this
app is made from can be read at
https://github.com/greyshirtguy/Pro7ChordEditor


HOW CHORDS ARE PUT ON

The first time the app is started it opens a guide: one page that says how
chords are put on, moved and taken off. After that the guide is in the Help
menu, and behind the ? at the right of the strip over a song.
